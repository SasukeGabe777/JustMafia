## Voice and AI models

- **Kokoro-82M** (hexgrad), Apache License 2.0 — the text-to-speech model that rendered the
  narrator clips in `public/audio/voice/` (tools/voice-gen.js) and that the optional
  "Natural live voice" setting runs in the browser. https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
- **kokoro-js** 1.2.1, Apache License 2.0 (per its npm metadata) — loaded from the jsDelivr CDN
  only when the optional live voice is switched on (it needs the internet to download the model
  anyway). https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro
- **Transformers.js / ONNX Runtime Web** (used inside kokoro-js), Apache-2.0 / MIT.

## Online services used by some games (not bundled)

These are contacted only when a game that uses them is played, and need the internet:

- Tune Buzz: song previews from the iTunes Search API (Apple) and Deezer API. Their terms
  apply; see docs/steam-release.md ("Outstanding legal risks").
- Funny Videos: YouTube, TikTok and Instagram embedded players. Their terms apply.
- The optional live voice: jsDelivr CDN (kokoro-js) and Hugging Face (model files).

## Music

The repository's `my-music/` folder holds songs added for testing. They are **not** included in
the desktop/Steam build unless their licences are confirmed (see docs/steam-release.md); players
can add their own songs in the app's music folder.
