# Third-party and AI-generated content

## Recorded music and sound effects (CC0 / free licenses)

Every recorded track and sound effect from other creators is listed with its author, source and
license in `public/audio/CREDITS.md` (generated by `tools/music/build-manifest.js`).

## Music (AI-generated, if any is shipped)

The recorded background music in `public/audio/music/` was **generated with AI**: the
open-source **ACE-Step 1.5** music model (ACE Studio and StepFun), run locally, then looped,
loudness-normalized and curated by us. Prompts, seeds and settings for every track are in
`tools/music/catalog.json` and `tools/music/picks.json`; the process is in `docs/music.md`.

- Model: ACE-Step 1.5 (`ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5`: `acestep-v15-turbo`, `acestep-5Hz-lm-1.7B`),
  https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5 and https://huggingface.co/ACE-Step/Ace-Step1.5
- License of the code and the weights: MIT. The model card states that generated music may be
  used commercially.
- Steam: declare the music as pre-generated AI content in the Steamworks content survey.

The synthesized scores (`public/box/scores/`, `public/music.js`), stingers and sound effects
are original code, not AI-generated audio.
