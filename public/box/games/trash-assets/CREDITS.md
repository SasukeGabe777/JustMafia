# JustZombies art and sound credits

Everything in this folder is **CC0 1.0 / public domain** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
CC0 needs no attribution, but we credit the authors anyway. Thank you!

Licenses were checked on each pack's or model's own page on 2026-11-25 (and on the bundled License.txt for Kenney packs).
No branded content, logos or real game characters are used.

What we did to them (also released as CC0): 3D models were rendered to isometric sprite atlases with one camera and
light rig (three.js in headless Chrome, `tools/trash-art-render/`), some recoloured, combined (props on heads and in
hands, blood pools under corpses) or animated (lids, the garage door, the rot nest). 2D particles were packed into one
atlas. Sounds were trimmed, normalised and re-encoded to OGG. A few objects are our own procedural models
(spike strip, sprinkler-flamer, car-alarm beacon, lawnmower, chainsaw, leaf blower, flamethrower, lightning coil,
ray gun, minigun, rocket sneakers, crown, bubble shield, binoculars, cash stacks, key piece, caged boss core, armour vest,
garage door, the rescue helicopter, the gunship, the jetpack, the hang-glider and the helipad).
